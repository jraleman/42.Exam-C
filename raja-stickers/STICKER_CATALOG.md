## 🖨️ Printing Guide

### Best Results:
1. **Paper**: Glossy sticker paper
2. **Settings**: 100% scale, no margins adjustment
3. **Quality**: Highest print quality
4. **Colors**: Full color (not grayscale)

### After Printing:
1. Let ink dry completely
2. Cut carefully along edges
3. Peel backing
4. Apply to clean, dry surface
